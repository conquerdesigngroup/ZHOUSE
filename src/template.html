Controls
  • Orbit drag to rotate · right-drag or Shift-drag to pan · scroll to zoom
  • Click a door to open or close it
  • Walk click the view, then use WASD or the arrow keys · move the mouse to look · Shift to run · E for doors
  • Touch drag the left side to move, the right side to look
  • Shift-click anything in 3D to select it for editing
Add
Snap to

Drag things in the plan to move them, and drag the round handles to resize. Doors and windows snap to the nearest wall. You can type lengths as 9.5, 9'6" or 114".

Electrical & HVAC

Pick a fixture, then click in the plan to place as many as you need; outlets and switches snap to the nearest wall. Esc when done. The Check tab tests outlet spacing, GFCI spots, switches and smoke alarms.

Furniture catalog

Click to add in the middle of the plan, or drag a card onto the plan. Sizes are typical real-world sizes; change them in the selection below.

Selection
Measure

Click two points in the 3D view: floor to ceiling, wall to wall, or the top of a counter. Each tape shows the straight distance plus how far across and how far up.

Spacing helpers

Select a piece of furniture to see the space it needs (teal is clear, red is short) and how far it sits from everything around it. Hold Alt while dragging to move freely.

Design check

Flags door swings that hit things, tight walkways, and fixture clearances while you edit. Click an item to jump to it in the plan.
